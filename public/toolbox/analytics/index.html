Setting up your site’s numbers

Two free Google tools tell you how your site is doing. Google Analytics shows who visits and what they do. Search Console shows how people find you on Google. You own both. You do a few short steps, then I handle the setup.

Time: about 20 minutesCost: freeYou’ll need: a Google account, and your domain already bought

Before you start

Use one Google account for both

Sign in with the Google account you want to own your business data. A Gmail address works. Use the same one for both tools, and keep it somewhere safe.

Owning these accounts means your numbers stay with you no matter who works on your site.

Part 1

Google Analytics

You create the account. I connect it to your site.

  1. Open Google Analytics

    Go to analytics.google.com. If it’s your first time, click Start measuring.

  2. Name the account

    Use your business name. Leave the data-sharing settings as they are, then click Next.

  3. Set up the property

    Name it after your website. Choose your time zone and currency, then click Next. Pick your industry and business size, choose how you plan to use Analytics, and click Create. Accept the terms.

  4. Stop at the data stream

    Google may ask you to set up a “data stream” next. You can close that screen. I’ll set it up and install it on your site.

  5. Add me

    Click Admin (the gear, bottom left). Under Account, open Access management. Click the + button, choose Add users, and enter analytics@omoniyistudio.com.

    Choose the Administrator role, then click Add. This lets me finish the setup. You stay the owner and can remove me anytime.

Part 2

Google Search Console

This one needs a little back and forth: you start it, I add a code to your domain, you finish it.

  1. Open Search Console

    Go to search.google.com/search-console with the same Google account.

  2. Add your domain

    Click Add property and choose Domain (the left option). Type your domain without https or www, like yourbusiness.com, then click Continue.

  3. Copy the code and send it to me

    Google shows a line of text that starts with google-site-verification=. Click Copy and email it to me. Leave that window open, or come back to it later.

  4. Click Verify when I say it’s in

    I’ll add the code to your domain and let you know. Then click Verify. If Google says it can’t find it yet, wait an hour and try again. It can take up to a day or two.

  5. Add me

    Once you’re verified, open Settings, then Users and permissions, and click Add user. Enter analytics@omoniyistudio.com, choose Full, and click Add.

After that

What happens next

I connect Analytics to your site and check that visits are coming through. Numbers start showing within a day, and Search Console takes a few days to fill in.

Once there’s data, I’ll walk you through the few numbers worth watching, so you don’t have to learn the whole dashboard.
