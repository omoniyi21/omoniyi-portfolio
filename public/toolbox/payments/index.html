Connecting your payments

For your website to take orders, I need access to the platform that handles your payments. Pick yours below. Your money always goes straight to you; I never touch payouts.

Time: about 10 minutesCost: freeYou’ll need: your Square login, on a computer

A heads-up on how much this opens. Today, the way to do this in Square is to add me to your team with full access. That lets me set up checkout for your site, and it also lets me see your sales and customers. I only use it for your website.

I’m building a one-click “Connect Square” button that asks only for what your site needs. When it’s ready, we’ll switch to it and you can remove me from your team.

Part 1

Add me to your team

  1. Open your team

    Sign in to your Square Dashboard on a computer. Go to Staff, then Team, then Team members, and click Add team member.

  2. Enter my details

    First name Omoniyi, last name Alimi, email hello@omoniyistudio.com. Click Next.

  3. Job details

    For the job title, type Website. I’m not on your payroll, so if Square asks about pay, choose any option and leave the amount at zero or blank. Click Next.

  4. Give me access

    Under access, choose Create a new permission set. Name it Website and choose the Full permission level. Pick your business location, then click Save.

  5. Leave the rest off

    Keep Authorized Representative turned off. If Square asks you to generate a passcode, go ahead; I won’t use it. Review and click Done.

After that

What happens next

I’ll get an invitation from Square and accept it. Then I connect checkout to your site and send you a test order to try before anything goes live.

You can remove me anytime: Staff, then Team, then Team members, select my name, and remove or deactivate me.
