Opening a room

Three steps

  1. Copy public/portal/6ix/ to public/portal/<project>/ and rewrite it for the client.
  2. In Netlify, add client@email.com=<project> to PORTAL_CLIENTS (comma between clients).
  3. Send them omoniyistudio.com/portal. They sign in with that email.
